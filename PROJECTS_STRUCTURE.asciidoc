= Projects structure

How the submodules under `projects/` are put together.
The book is written against two domains, `heads-of-governments` and `mysql-sakila`, and every submodule belongs to one
of them, as `<domain>-<part>`.
The versions every part is built on are set in link:PROJECTS_VERSIONS.asciidoc[].

== Parts

`<domain>-persistence`::
The Jakarta Persistence mapping of the domain's database.
Every domain has it, and a domain may have nothing else.
It is plain Jakarta Persistence, so it runs on each persistence provider it has a profile for.

`<domain>-spring-data`::
Spring Data JPA repositories over the domain's `-persistence` mapping.
Optional.
It depends on `<domain>-persistence` (and on its `test-companion` artifact for tests), and runs on Hibernate ORM, the
provider Spring Boot wires.

`<domain>-jakarta-data`::
Jakarta Data repositories over the domain's `-persistence` mapping.
Optional, and independent of `-spring-data`: a domain may have either, both or neither.
It depends on `<domain>-persistence`, and runs on Hibernate ORM, since the repositories are generated by Hibernate's
processor.

A domain's database lives in its `-persistence` module, or outside the projects: the SQLite file of
`heads-of-governments-persistence`, with its schema and diagrams, is under its `db/`; the `sakila` database is MySQL's
own sample, which `mysql-sakila-persistence` runs in Docker.
There is no separate database part.

== Weld

A part may test in a CDI container, with Weld SE and weld-junit.
`mysql-sakila-jakarta-data` does: its generated repositories take their `EntityManagerFactory` from a
`@PersistenceUnit` field, as in an application server, and a test picks the persistence unit (`test`, on H2, or
`it`, on MySQL) by the producer class it hands the container.
The weld-junit artifact depends on the generation; see the note under Jakarta EE 12 in
link:PROJECTS_VERSIONS.asciidoc[].
