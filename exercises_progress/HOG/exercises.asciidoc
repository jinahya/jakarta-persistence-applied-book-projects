= Exercises
:toc: left
:toclevels: 3

The exercises on `src/main/resources/heads_of_governments.sqlite3`: a section for each table, with a section for each level, and, at the end, a section for the schema as a whole.
Each exercise states the request it answers; the SQL that answers it is in the `.sql` file, under the same id.
The ids are registered in `EXERCISES.yaml`.

Parameters are bind variables (`:name`); the `sqlite3` shell binds one with `.parameter set :name 'value'`, and an IDE prompts for them when a statement runs.
An exercise with a parameter, or one that measures to `date('now')`, shows no output, since what it gives back depends on the value or the day.

Run a file as a whole:

[source,sh]
----
sqlite3 -readonly src/main/resources/heads_of_governments.sqlite3 < src/test/sql-exercises/<section>/<NN>_<level>.sql
----

== head

The `head` table: a row per term of a head of government.

=== Basic

[[HOG_HEAD_B_01]]
==== HOG_HEAD_B_01: Who held office on a day

A reader gives a date and wants each country's head on that day.
A country whose office was vacant is absent.

.`head/01_basic.sql`, HOG_HEAD_B_01
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT *
FROM head
WHERE term_start <= :date
  AND (term_end IS NULL OR term_end >= :date)
ORDER BY country ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_I_01]]
==== HOG_HEAD_I_01: The longest terms of a country

For one country, each term with its length in days, longest first; the incumbent's term runs to today.

.`head/02_intermediate.sql`, HOG_HEAD_I_01
[%collapsible]

[[HOG_HEAD_I_03]]
==== HOG_HEAD_I_03: Age on taking office

Per country, the youngest, the oldest and the average age at which a term began, in whole years of the calendar (the year of term_start less the year of birth; born is a year, so that is as fine as the data allows).

.`head/02_intermediate.sql`, HOG_HEAD_I_03
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT g.*,
       MIN(CAST(SUBSTR(h.term_start, 1, 4) AS INTEGER) - h.born)           AS youngest,
       MAX(CAST(SUBSTR(h.term_start, 1, 4) AS INTEGER) - h.born)           AS oldest,
       ROUND(AVG(CAST(SUBSTR(h.term_start, 1, 4) AS INTEGER) - h.born), 1) AS average
FROM head h
         JOIN office g ON g.country = h.country
GROUP BY g.country
ORDER BY g.country ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_I_08]]
==== HOG_HEAD_I_08: The first and the latest terms of each country

Per country, its first :n terms and its most recent :n, each row saying which it is.
A row is 'first' when it is among the first :n, so with a country of 2 * :n terms or fewer the two sets overlap and 'first' wins.

.`head/02_intermediate.sql`, HOG_HEAD_I_08
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT *,
       CASE WHEN first_rn <= :n THEN 'first' ELSE 'latest' END AS which
FROM (SELECT h.*,
             ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start ASC)  AS first_rn, ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start DESC) AS latest_rn
      FROM head AS h)
WHERE first_rn <= :n
   OR latest_rn <= :n
ORDER BY country ASC, term_start ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_I_09]]
==== HOG_HEAD_I_09: The first two terms of each country

[[HOG_HEAD_I_10]]
==== HOG_HEAD_I_10: Two or more terms, the top three per country

Per country, up to three heads with two or more terms, most terms first and, at a tie, the earlier first term first; a name is taken as one person.

.`head/02_intermediate.sql`, HOG_HEAD_I_10
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT country, name, terms
FROM (SELECT country,
             name,
             COUNT(*) AS  terms,
             ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY COUNT(*) DESC, MIN(term_start) ASC) AS place
      FROM head
      GROUP BY country, name
      HAVING COUNT(*) >= 2)
WHERE place <= 3
ORDER BY country ASC, place ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_I_11]]
==== HOG_HEAD_I_11: Party share of completed terms, the top three per country

Per country, over the terms that have ended, each party's share of the days in office as a percentage of that country's total (its tenure share), the top three parties; a succession ('A/B') counts as a party of its own.

.`head/02_intermediate.sql`, HOG_HEAD_I_11
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT country, party, tenure_share
FROM (SELECT country,
             party,
             ROUND(100.0 * SUM(julianday(term_end) - julianday(term_start))
                       / SUM(SUM(julianday(term_end) - julianday(term_start))) OVER (PARTITION BY country),
                   1) AS  tenure_share,
             ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY country
                 ORDER BY SUM(julianday(term_end) - julianday(term_start)) DESC)             AS place
      FROM head
      WHERE term_end IS NOT NULL
      GROUP BY country, party)
WHERE place <= 3
ORDER BY country ASC, tenure_share DESC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_I_12]]

[[HOG_HEAD_I_13]]
==== HOG_HEAD_I_13: Terms begun per day of the week

Across all countries, how many terms began on each day of the week, Sunday first. strftime('%w', ...) gives the day as a digit, Sunday as 0; a CASE turns it into a name.

.`head/02_intermediate.sql`, HOG_HEAD_I_13
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT CASE strftime('%w', term_start)
           WHEN '0' THEN 'Sunday'
           WHEN '1' THEN 'Monday'
           WHEN '2' THEN 'Tuesday'
           WHEN '3' THEN 'Wednesday'
           WHEN '4' THEN 'Thursday'
           WHEN '5' THEN 'Friday'
           ELSE 'Saturday'
           END  AS weekday,
       COUNT(*) AS terms
FROM head
GROUP BY strftime('%w', term_start)
ORDER BY strftime('%w', term_start) ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_I_14]]

[[HOG_HEAD_I_16]]
==== HOG_HEAD_I_16: Overlapping terms in a country

Within a country, a term should not begin before the one before it has ended.
List each pair of terms of a country where the later begins before the earlier ends, an open term running to '9999-12-31', by country, then by the earlier term; the check passes when nothing comes back.
A handover day, the end of one term and the start of the next, is not an overlap.

.`head/02_intermediate.sql`, HOG_HEAD_I_16
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT a.*, b.id AS later_id, b.name AS later_name, b.term_start AS later_term_start
FROM head a
         JOIN head b ON b.country = a.country
    AND b.term_start > a.term_start
    AND b.term_start < COALESCE(a.term_end, '9999-12-31')
ORDER BY a.country ASC, a.term_start ASC, b.term_start ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_I_17]]
==== HOG_HEAD_I_17: Terms begun fifteen years younger than the country's average

The terms whose head, in years of the calendar, was fifteen or more years younger than the average age at which that country's terms began, by country, then in date order.
A correlated subquery gives each term its own country's average.

.`head/02_intermediate.sql`, HOG_HEAD_I_17
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT h.*, CAST(SUBSTR(h.term_start, 1, 4) AS INTEGER) - h.born AS age
FROM head h
WHERE CAST(SUBSTR(h.term_start, 1, 4) AS INTEGER) - h.born
          <= (SELECT AVG(CAST(SUBSTR(s.term_start, 1, 4) AS INTEGER) - s.born)
              FROM head s
              WHERE s.country = h.country) - 15
ORDER BY h.country ASC, h.term_start ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_A_01]]
==== HOG_HEAD_A_01: Vacancies

Per country, each gap of more than a day between a term's end and the next term's start, with its length; a day between is only how some sources write a succession, so it does not count.

.`head/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_HEAD_A_01
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT *,
       CAST(julianday(term_start) - julianday(previous_term_end) AS INTEGER) - 1 AS days_vacant
FROM (SELECT h.*,
             LAG(name) OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start)     AS previous_name, LAG(term_end) OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start) AS previous_term_end
      FROM head AS h)
WHERE julianday(term_start) - julianday(previous_term_end) > 1
ORDER BY country ASC, term_start ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_A_02]]
==== HOG_HEAD_A_02: Longest in office

Per country, the person with the most days in office over all their terms, counting an open term to today; ties share the place.

.`head/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_HEAD_A_02
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT country, name, terms, days
FROM (SELECT country,
             name,
             COUNT(*)                                                                                  AS terms,
             CAST(SUM(julianday(COALESCE(term_end, date ('now'))) - julianday(term_start)) AS INTEGER) AS days,
             RANK()                                                                                       OVER (PARTITION BY country
                 ORDER BY SUM(julianday(COALESCE(term_end, date('now'))) - julianday(term_start)) DESC) AS place
      FROM head
      GROUP BY country, name)
WHERE place = 1
ORDER BY country ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_A_03]]
==== HOG_HEAD_A_03: The longest run of one party

Per country, the longest unbroken run of consecutive terms held by the same party, with when it began and ended; a run is counted in terms, not days, and a succession ('UNR/UDR') is a party of its own.

.`head/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_HEAD_A_03
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
WITH ordered AS (SELECT country,
                        party,
                        term_start,
                        term_end,
                        ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start)        AS seq, ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY country, party ORDER BY term_start) AS seq_in_party
                 FROM head),
     runs AS (SELECT country,
                     party,
                     COUNT(*)                              AS terms,
                     MIN(term_start)                       AS since,
                     MAX(COALESCE(term_end, date ('now'))) AS until
              FROM ordered
              GROUP BY country, party, seq - seq_in_party),
     placed AS (SELECT *, RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY terms DESC, since ASC) AS place
                FROM runs)
SELECT country, party, terms, since, until
FROM placed
WHERE place = 1
ORDER BY country ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_A_04]]
==== HOG_HEAD_A_04: Every party of a head, one per row

party may be a succession, 'A/B', when the party was renamed or changed under the head.
Give the heads whose party is one, with each party on its own row and its position in the succession.

.`head/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_HEAD_A_04
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
WITH RECURSIVE parts(id, country, name, term_start, position, party, rest) AS
                   (SELECT id, country, name, term_start, 0, NULL, party || '/'
                    FROM head
                    WHERE party LIKE '%/%'
                    UNION ALL
                    SELECT id,
                           country,
                           name,
                           term_start,
                           position + 1,
                           SUBSTR(rest, 1, INSTR(rest, '/') - 1),
                           SUBSTR(rest, INSTR(rest, '/') + 1)
                    FROM parts
                    WHERE rest <> '')
SELECT country, name, term_start, position, party
FROM parts
WHERE position > 0
ORDER BY country ASC, term_start ASC, position ASC
----

[[HOG_HEAD_A_06]]
==== HOG_HEAD_A_06: How often a new head brings a new party

Per country, over the successions where the head changed (not a head following themselves), how many also changed the party, and that as a percentage, by country code.
LAG gives the previous term's name and party; a succession ('A/B') counts as a party of its own.

.`head/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_HEAD_A_06
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT g.*,
       COUNT(*)                                                      AS successions,
       SUM(h.party <> h.previous_party)                              AS party_changes,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(h.party <> h.previous_party) / COUNT(*), 1) AS change_share
FROM (SELECT country,
             name,
             party,
             LAG(name) OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start)  AS previous_name,
             LAG(party) OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start) AS previous_party
      FROM head) h
         JOIN office g ON g.country = h.country
WHERE h.previous_name IS NOT NULL
  AND h.name <> h.previous_name
GROUP BY g.country
ORDER BY g.country ASC
----


====

[[HOG_HEAD_A_07]]
==== HOG_HEAD_A_07: Comebacks

A stint is an unbroken run of terms by one person; a head who left and came back has more than one.
Give the heads with three stints or more, with the year each stint began, most stints first and, at a tie, by country, then by the first term.
A term starts a stint when the term before it in that country was someone else's.

.`head/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_HEAD_A_07
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
WITH marked AS (SELECT country,
                       name,
                       term_start,
                       LAG(name) OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start) AS previous_name
                FROM head)
SELECT country,
       name,
       COUNT(*)                                                         AS stints,
       GROUP_CONCAT(SUBSTR(term_start, 1, 4), ', ' ORDER BY term_start) AS stint_years
FROM marked
WHERE previous_name IS NULL
   OR previous_name <> name
GROUP BY country, name
HAVING COUNT(*) >= 3
ORDER BY stints DESC, country ASC, MIN(term_start) ASC
----


====

[[HOG_HEAD_A_08]]
==== HOG_HEAD_A_08: Living heads who outlived their successor

The terms of heads still living whose immediate successor, someone else, has died, with the successor and the year of their death, by country, then by the term.
died is NULL for the living, so the test is IS NULL on the head and IS NOT NULL on the successor; comparing the two years would give NULL, and no row, for exactly these heads.

.`head/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_HEAD_A_08
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT country, number, name, term_start, term_end, successor, successor_died
FROM (SELECT h.*,
             LEAD(name) OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start) AS successor,
             LEAD(died) OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start) AS successor_died
      FROM head AS h)
WHERE died IS NULL
  AND successor <> name
  AND successor_died IS NOT NULL
ORDER BY country ASC, term_start ASC
----

=== Advanced

[[HOG_OFFICE_A_01]]
==== HOG_OFFICE_A_01: What number counts, in a phrase

numbering is a sentence, but each opens by naming what is counted -- 'ordinal of the person by first election', '代', '대', 'presidency number' -- before its first comma or semicolon.
Show each office with that phrase alone.

.`office/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_OFFICE_A_01
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT *,
       CASE
           WHEN comma > 0 AND (semicolon = 0 OR comma < semicolon) THEN SUBSTR(numbering, 1, comma - 1)
           WHEN semicolon > 0 THEN SUBSTR(numbering, 1, semicolon - 1)
           ELSE numbering
           END AS counts
FROM (SELECT *, INSTR(numbering, ',') AS comma, INSTR(numbering, ';') AS semicolon
      FROM office)
ORDER BY country ASC
----

[[HOG_SCHEMA_I_06]]
==== HOG_SCHEMA_I_06: Age at first taking office, by the kind of head

By the kind of head: how many persons, and the youngest, the oldest and the average age at which each first took office, in years of the calendar.
Each person counts once, at their first term, unlike HOG_HEAD_I_03, which counts every term; a name is taken as one person within a country.

.`__/02_intermediate.sql`, HOG_SCHEMA_I_06
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT g.head_kind,
       COUNT(*)                                                             AS persons,
       MIN(CAST(SUBSTR(f.first_start, 1, 4) AS INTEGER) - f.born)           AS youngest,
       MAX(CAST(SUBSTR(f.first_start, 1, 4) AS INTEGER) - f.born)           AS oldest,
       ROUND(AVG(CAST(SUBSTR(f.first_start, 1, 4) AS INTEGER) - f.born), 1) AS average
FROM office g
         JOIN (SELECT country, name, born, MIN(term_start) AS first_start
               FROM head
               GROUP BY country, name, born) f ON f.country = g.country
GROUP BY g.head_kind
ORDER BY g.head_kind ASC
----

==== HOG_SCHEMA_I_07: The shortest completed term of each office

Per office, with its title, the completed term with the fewest days and who held it, by country code; at a tie, every such term, the earlier first.

.`__/02_intermediate.sql`, HOG_SCHEMA_I_07

[[HOG_SCHEMA_A_04]]
==== HOG_SCHEMA_A_04: Each office's longest-lived head

Per office, with its title, the head who lived the most years (died less born) among those who have died; ties share the place and are listed by name.

.`__/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_SCHEMA_A_04
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]

FROM (SELECT DISTINCT g.country,
                      g.head_title,
                      h.name,
                      h.born,
                      h.died,
                      h.died - h.born                                                    AS age,
                      RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY g.country ORDER BY h.died - h.born DESC) AS place
      FROM office g
               JOIN head h ON h.country = g.country

WHERE place = 1
ORDER BY country ASC, name ASC

[[HOG_SCHEMA_A_05]]
==== HOG_SCHEMA_A_05: Presidents and the prime ministers of their time

For each president's term, how many different prime ministers, of any office, were in office at some point during it; the ten terms with the most first and, at a tie, the earlier term first, then by country.
Two terms overlap when each starts on or before the other's end, the handover day counting for both; an open term ends at '9999-12-31'.

.`__/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_SCHEMA_A_05
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT p.*, COUNT(DISTINCT m.country || ' ' || m.name) AS prime_ministers
FROM head p
         JOIN office pg ON pg.country = p.country AND pg.head_kind = 'PRESIDENT'
         JOIN head m ON m.term_start <= COALESCE(p.term_end, '9999-12-31')
    AND COALESCE(m.term_end, '9999-12-31') >= p.term_start
         JOIN office mg ON mg.country = m.country AND mg.head_kind = 'PRIME_MINISTER'
GROUP BY p.id
ORDER BY prime_ministers DESC, p.term_start ASC, p.country ASC
LIMIT 10
----

[[HOG_SCHEMA_A_06]]
==== HOG_SCHEMA_A_06: New heads per century, with a running total

Per office, with its title, how many persons first took office in each century, and the running total of persons up to that century, by country, then by century.
The century is the first two digits of the year plus one, so 1800 counts in the 19th; a name is taken as one person within a country.

.`__/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_SCHEMA_A_06
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
WITH firsts AS (SELECT country, name, MIN(term_start) AS first_start
                FROM head
                GROUP BY country, name),
     per_century AS (SELECT g.country,
                            g.head_title,
                            CAST(SUBSTR(f.first_start, 1, 2) AS INTEGER) + 1 AS century,
                            COUNT(*)                                         AS new_persons
                     FROM office g
                              JOIN firsts f ON f.country = g.country
                     GROUP BY g.country, g.head_title, century)
SELECT country,
       head_title,
       century,
       new_persons,
       SUM(new_persons) OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY century) AS persons_so_far
FROM per_century
ORDER BY country ASC, century ASC
----

==== HOG_SCHEMA_A_07: Decades in which every office changed hands

The decades in which a term began in every office, with how many terms began in all, oldest first.
This is relational division: a decade qualifies when the number of distinct countries with a term begun in it equals the number of offices.

.`__/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_SCHEMA_A_07
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----

[[HOG_SCHEMA_A_08]]
==== HOG_SCHEMA_A_08: The median completed term of each office

Per office, with its title, how many terms have ended and the median of their lengths in days, cut to whole days, by country code.
The median is the middle length, or the average of the two middle ones when the count is even: ROW_NUMBER numbers the lengths, COUNT(*) OVER gives the count n, and the middle rows are (n + 1) / 2 and (n + 2) / 2 in integer division, one row when n is odd.

.`__/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_SCHEMA_A_08
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
WITH lengths AS (SELECT country,
                        julianday(term_end) - julianday(term_start)                     AS days,
                        ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY country
                            ORDER BY julianday(term_end) - julianday(term_start)) AS rn,
                        COUNT(*) OVER (PARTITION BY country)                            AS n
                 FROM head
                 WHERE term_end IS NOT NULL)
SELECT g.country, g.head_title, l.n AS terms, CAST(AVG(l.days) AS INTEGER) AS median_days
FROM office g
         JOIN lengths l ON l.country = g.country
WHERE l.rn IN ((l.n + 1) / 2, (l.n + 2) / 2)
GROUP BY g.country, g.head_title, l.n
ORDER BY g.country ASC
----
